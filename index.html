Sheet to Spotify

Paste a music planning sheet and get one Spotify playlist, in the same order as the event.

To install on iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

Open in Spotify

Load a sheet to see its songs here, grouped by section. Each song turns green, yellow, or red as it's matched on Spotify.

Green: added. Yellow: added, but check the version. Red: not found, add it by hand in Spotify. Grey: duplicate, skipped. Nothing is stored except your login, on this device.
